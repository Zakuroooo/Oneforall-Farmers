## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-trust, high-contrast, tactile marketplace experience tailored for agricultural commerce under harsh field conditions. It rejects the bureaucratic aesthetic of state portals and generic fintech blue in favor of an earth-rooted, hyper-polished consumer interface inspired by world-class on-demand logistics apps.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Impact
- **Authoritative & Trustworthy:** Communicates financial transparency, accurate pricing per quintal, and instant settlement clarity.
- **Rooted & Tactile:** Draws visceral cues from physical grain and vegetable auction yards (mandi sheds, jute sacks, brass weighing scales, and sun-baked terracotta tile).
- **Daylight Resilient:** Built explicitly for outdoor read-write operations in bright, glare-heavy sunlight with uncompromising contrast ratios and deep ink densities.

### Design Movement
**Tactile Modernism with High Contrast.** Combines dense tabular information architecture with physical, touchable surface elevation. Elements use crisp 1.5px structural borders, rich sunlit shadows, and saturated harvest ochres over parchment surfaces to produce immediate physical hierarchy.

## Colors

The color palette reflects agricultural abundance, physical earth, and trade momentum. All tonal pairings exceed WCAG AAA standards for text on core daylight displays.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (Deep Marigold / Warm Ochre - `#C2410C`):** Primary action drivers, auction bid confirmations, active tab indicators, and progress states. Accents scale up to vibrant Turmeric (`#D96B00`) and Marigold (`#E65100`) for interactive alerts and touch focus states.
- **Secondary (Raw Terracotta - `#9A3412`):** Structural containers, commodity category filters (e.g., Nashik Red Onion, Latur Grade-A Soybean), headers, and high-emphasis meta badges.
- **Tertiary (Verified Emerald - `#047857`):** Real-time APMC mandi rate surges, successful Escrow releases, QC Grade-A verifications, and net-positive trading margins.
- **Critical Downside (Honest Crimson - `#B91C1C`):** Price drops per quintal, moisture content rejection warnings, payment dispute triggers, and perishable risk countdowns.
- **Neutral Background (Warm Jute Parchment - `#FAF6EE`):** Non-glare canvas base that prevents eye fatigue under outdoor daylight while preserving warm, physical depth.
- **Neutral Foreground (Charcoal Black - `#18181B`):** Deep carbon ink for primary typography, tabular pricing, and weight figures, ensuring maximum optical density.
- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Canvas: `#FAF6EE`
  - Card/Container Surface: `#FFFFFF`
  - Secondary Inset/Field Plate: `#F3EDE0`
  - Deep Neutral Slate: `#27272A`

## Typography

The typography leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to combine geometric precision with warm humanist aperture openings, ensuring instant recognition for vernacular speakers and field operators.

### Numerical Legibility & Tabular Alignment
- **Tabular Lining Numerals:** Every currency display (`₹`), metric weight figure (`Qtl`, `Kg`), and moisture percentage (`%`) must enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1`. This prevents layout shifts during live APMC auction bidding tickers.
- **High-Density Legibility:** Data-dense rows use medium to bold weights (`600`/`700`) even at 13px–14px to counteract ambient light washouts.
- **Multilingual Balance:** Generous line heights are maintained across titles and body copy to accommodate Marathi and Hindi diacritics without clipping top matras or base conjuncts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around direct thumb reach, outdoor hand ergonomics, and dense commodity status overview. It employs a dynamic fluid grid system grounded on a strict **4px baseline / 8px core grid**.

### Touch Ergonomics & Form Factors
- **Mobile First (4–Column Fluid):**
  - Breakpoint: `< 640px`
  - Screen Margin: `16px`, Gutter: `12px`
  - Bottom-anchored action docks for all high-intent bids, truck dispatch calls, and trade confirmations.
  - Critical touch-points enforce a minimum height of `48px` (`touch-target-min`), escalating to `56px` (`touch-target-hero`) for primary bid buttons.
- **Tablet / Mandi Trader Pad (8–Column Fluid):**
  - Breakpoint: `640px – 1024px`
  - Screen Margin: `24px`, Gutter: `16px`
  - Master-detail split: Left rail displays live commodity lots (Onion/Soybean), right side hosts auction ladder and logistics details.
- **Desktop / Yard Manager Console (12–Column Fixed Grid):**
  - Breakpoint: `> 1024px` (Max content container: `1200px`)
  - Screen Margin: `32px`, Gutter: `24px`
  - High-throughput weighbridge and escrow settlement tables.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical stacking metaphors, low-glare warm shadows, and micro-borders to maintain distinction on sun-faded mobile displays.

### Depth Architecture
1. **Level 0 (Parchment Foundation):**
   - Background canvas: `#FAF6EE`. Flat, non-reflective.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Lot Containers):**
   - Solid `#FFFFFF` fill with a `1px` structural outline in `#EADEC7` (jute fiber border).
   - Shadow: `0 2px 4px rgba(154, 52, 18, 0.04), 0 1px 2px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.06)`.
3. **Level 2 (Active Bidding Cards, Live Statuses & Dropdowns):**
   - Solid `#FFFFFF` fill with a `1.5px` border in `#C2410C` (primary ochre) when focused or active.
   - Shadow: `0 6px 16px -2px rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.12), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.08)`.
4. **Level 3 (Sticky Bottom Trays & Floating Triggers):**
   - Top-edge border: `1.5px solid #E5D9C3`.
   - Shadow: `0 -4px 20px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.09)`.
5. **Level 4 (Modals, Sliders, OTP Sheets):**
   - Heavy diffused grounding shadow: `0 20px 32px -4px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.22)`.
   - Backdrop overlay: `#18181B` at `55%` opacity with a subtle `2px` blur for optical focus.

## Shapes

The design system employs a calibrated **Rounded (Level 2)** shape identity. Curves avoid clinical tech roundness in favor of firm, organic corners that evoke physical stamps, grain bags, and card vouchers.

### Radius Assignments
- **Core Cards & Containers (`rounded-lg`):** `1rem` (16px). Balances approachable ergonomics with dense horizontal layout space.
- **Inputs, Bid Steppers, & Action Buttons (`rounded-md`):** `0.75rem` (12px). Creates decisive, tactile targets under thumb interaction.
- **Badges, APMC Mandi Tickers, & Status Pills (`rounded-full`):** `9999px`. Distinguishes ephemeral dynamic indicators (e.g., "Mandi Open", "Grade A1") from tappable surfaces.
- **Bottom Sheets & Modal Top Rims (`rounded-t-xl`):** `1.5rem` (24px). Provides an anchor for pull-up operational sheets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Commit Bid / Accept Price):**
  - Height: `52px` (mobile minimum) or `56px` (docked). Solid `#C2410C` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `title-lg` typography.
  - Active State: Translates down `1px` with an inner shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)`), offering haptic tactile feedback.
- **Secondary Action (Counter-Offer / View Weighment Slip):**
  - Height: `48px`. Pure `#FFFFFF` background with a crisp `1.5px` border in `#9A3412`, `#9A3412` label.
- **Tertiary / Utility (Voice Input / Share WhatsApp Mandi Receipt):**
  - Fill: `#F3EDE0`, text: `#18181B`, border: `1px solid transparent`.
  - Icon-right alignment for rapid contextual scanning.

### Chips & Filter Tabs
- **Mandi Variety Selectors (e.g., "Lasalgaon Onion", "Pimpalgaon Soy"):**
  - Height: `38px`. Default state: `#FFFFFF` fill, `#4B5563` text, `1px solid #E2D7C2`.
  - Selected state: `#9A3412` background, `#FFFFFF` bold text, high-contrast drop-shadow.
- **Metric Micro-Chips (Moisture %, Foreign Matter):**
  - Fixed-height `26px` pills with `#FAF6EE` background, Charcoal `#18181B` tabular numbers, and border accent matching status (`#047857` for acceptable, `#B91C1C` for excess moisture).

### Cards (Commodity Auction Lot Card)
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` with `16px` border radius and `1px solid #EADEC7`.
- **Top Bar:** Crisp lot reference number in `label-sm` with a right-aligned verification badge (e.g., `#047857` badge: "Assayed & Weighed").
- **Middle Grid:** Two-column split. Left column showcases commodity avatar with crop badge ("Onion - Gavran Red"); right column features tabular price per quintal (`numeral-hero` style: `₹2,850/Qtl`).
- **Footer Shelf:** Integrated `#FAF6EE` lower tier containing distance (`"14 km away • Dindori"`), total lot value, and direct Call Mandi Agent button.

### Form Inputs & Price Steppers
- **Price Increment Stepper (Custom Mandi Bid Engine):**
  - Container with a `52px` height, `#FFFFFF` background, and `2px solid #C2410C` outline.
  - Large minus and plus hit areas (`48x48px`) flanking a center tabular readout (`display: inline-block; font-size: 20px; font-weight: 800; color: #18181B`).
- **Text & Numeric Input Fields:**
  - Inactive state: `1.5px solid #D6C7B2`, `#FAF6EE` interior.
  - Focus state: `2px solid #C2410C`, `#FFFFFF` interior, explicit floating label in `#9A3412`.

### Lists & Live Mandi Tickers
- **APMC Market Ladder Rows:**
  - Alternating rows using `#FFFFFF` and `#FAF6EE` to facilitate scanning without eye jump.
  - Left: Mandi Name + Distance. Right: High-contrast price with directional trend icons (Crisp `#047857` up-arrow for rising prices; `#B91C1C` down-arrow for cooling trends).
  - Minimum row height: `56px`.

### Selection Controls (Radio & Checkbox)
- Checkboxes: `22x22px` box, `2px solid #9A3412`, filled with `#9A3412` and a white checkmark when active.
- Radio buttons: `24x24px` ring with a solid `#C2410C` inner disc (`12px`) on white canvas.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Weighbridge & Moisture Diagnostic Card:** Tactile visual meter showing allowable soybean moisture threshold (up to 10%) vs. current sample moisture (12.4%), using crisp Emerald and Crimson color boundaries.
- **Escrow Guarantee Lock Banner:** High-visibility `#F3EDE0` band with a verified lock icon, ensuring funds are deposited with the Mandi Board before the truck departs the farm gate.
